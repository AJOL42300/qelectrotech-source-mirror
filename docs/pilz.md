# Pilz safety switches and cables

Vector elements (schematic symbol and realistic view) for eight Pilz order
numbers, in
`extra-elements/19_pilz`. They are generated by
`misc/pilz/gen_pilz.py` (helper: `misc/pilz/qetlib.py`) and checked by
`tests/qttest/tst_pilzelements.cpp`.

```sh
python misc/pilz/gen_pilz.py        # rewrites extra-elements/19_pilz
```

Every drawing is original. No manufacturer image or text is copied; the
photos only served as a reference for the shape. Each element carries
`manufacturer` = Pilz, `manufacturer_reference` = the order number and
a description that states which data is verified.

Status labels used below:

- **VERIFIED**: read in a public Pilz document (URL given).
- **ASSUMED**: deduced, not stated for this exact order number.

For the cables, the terminals on the left are the connector pins
(`1`, `2`, ...) and the terminals on the right are the wire ends, named with
the IEC 60757 colour code. The wire is drawn as a stripe in its colour
(two-colour wires are striped).

## Realistic views

Every element has a second appearance, `<element>_realistic.elmt`, in the
same folder (name ending in "realistic view" / "vue réaliste"). It has the
same terminals (names, positions, orientations), the same manufacturer
information and the same pin table data as the schematic symbol, so both can
be used for the same device. All elements are `link_type="master"` with
one `plcIO` entry per terminal (supply and wire ends as universal input,
inputs and outputs of the PSENmgate and the safety outputs as digital
input/output; the entry holds the function or the pin).

The views are original vector drawings, with no image and no manufacturer
text:

- PSENmgate: housing seen from the front with two illuminated pushbuttons,
  the E-STOP, the cable outlet below and a dark coding slot on the left, right
  or front according to the order number; coloured, tagged wires lead to the
  terminals (the reserved pins 8 and 17 have no terminal).
- PSEN cs1.1n: switch head with the M12 connector below; wires of the
  accessory cable in the colours of the pin-out.
- PSEN ml 1.1: tapered tongue and head with two fixing holes.
- Cables: connector (M12 or M23) with the pins fanned in, the yellow cable and
  the wires fanned out in their colour.

ASSUMED (illustrative, not taken from a Pilz drawing): the colours of the
pushbuttons and of the LED, the exact proportions and the place of the
coding slot of each PSENmgate variant. The pin-outs and wire colours stay
as in the VERIFIED / ASSUMED statements below.

## Pin-outs and wire colours

### 540003 PSEN cs1.1n (5-pin M12 male) and cable 630311

| Pin | Terminal | Function | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A1 | +24 V DC supply UB | BN brown |
| 2 | 12 | safety output 1 | WH white |
| 3 | A2 | 0 V supply UB | BU blue |
| 4 | 22 | safety output 2 | BK black |
| 5 | none | do not connect | GY grey |

- VERIFIED: the function of each pin (PSEN cs1.1n operating manual,
  https://www.manualslib.com/guide/4239823 and Pilz e-shop page of 540003,
  https://www.pilz.com/en-INT/eshop/product/540003).
- VERIFIED: the cable 630311 has five wires brown, white, blue, black,
  grey (PSEN op cable M12 5p data sheet 1002482-EN-05,
  https://cdn.logic-control.com/docs/pilz-safety/Manuals/PSEN_op_Cable_M12_5p_Data_sh_1002482-EN-05.pdf).
- ASSUMED: the pin-to-colour order of 630311. It is the A-coded M12
  standard order (EN 61076-2-101) and it matches the cs1.1n manual.

### 630311 PSEN op cable axial M12 5-pole, 5 m

VERIFIED (data sheet A700000013012462,
https://docs.rs-online.com/41e2/A700000013012462.pdf): PUR, yellow, M12
female straight A-coded, open line end, 5 x 0.25 mm2 (AWG24), unshielded,
diameter 5.1 mm, 4 A.

### C1000073 (5 m) and C1000074 (10 m): 19-pin M23 cable

VERIFIED (Pilz data sheets,
https://pim.galco.com/Manufacturer/Pilz/TechDocument/Data%20Sheet/C1000073.pdf
and `.../C1000074.pdf`): M23 19-pin female connector, straight, 19 x 0.25 mm2
(AWG24), unshielded, PUR, yellow (RAL 1003), 8.05 mm, 2 A.

| Pin | Colour | Pin | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | RD red | 11 | YEBN yellow-brown |
| 2 | GY grey | 12 | GNYE green-yellow |
| 3 | VT violet | 13 | BK black |
| 4 | YE yellow | 14 | WHYE white-yellow |
| 5 | GN green | 15 | WHGN white-green |
| 6 | BU blue | 16 | WHGY white-grey |
| 7 | WH white | 17 | GYPK grey-pink |
| 8 | RDBU red-blue | 18 | PK pink |
| 9 | GYBN grey-brown | 19 | BN brown |
| 10 | BNGN brown-green | | |

- VERIFIED: the colours are in the "Cable colour (Pilz cable)" column of the
  pin table of the PSENmgate PSEN mg1-A-LLFXX operating manual 1006672-EN-06
  (https://www.manualslib.com/manual/4359412/Pilz-Psen-Mg1-A-Llfxx-Series.html,
  pages 24-25).
- ASSUMED: those colours apply to C1000073 and C1000074. The data sheets do
  not list the colours.
- ASSUMED: the far end is an open line end. The data sheets show one
  connector only.
- Pilz calls the connector "N-coded" in the data sheet. The wording is kept
  as given and was not checked against the standard.

### 6M000007 / 6M000008 / 6M000009 PSENmgate PSEN mg1-A-LLFXX

Two illuminated pushbuttons (1 NO each) and one emergency stop
(2 NC + 1 NO), coded, automatic reset, 19-pin M23 male connector, 24 V DC.
The entry side is the only difference: left (6M000007), right (6M000008),
front (6M000009). Actuator: PSEN ml 1.1 (570480).

| Pin | Terminal | Colour | Pin | Terminal | Colour |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Y1 | RD | 11 | ES1.2 | YEBN |
| 2 | Y32 | GY | 12 | B1 | GNYE |
| 3 | ES_SO | VT | 13 | LED2 | BK |
| 4 | 22 | YE | 14 | ES2.1 | WHYE |
| 5 | 12 | GN | 15 | LED1 | WHGN |
| 6 | A2 | BU | 16 | ES2.2 | WHGY |
| 7 | S41 | WH | 17 | reserved | GYPK |
| 8 | reserved | RDBU | 18 | S31 | PK |
| 9 | ES1.1 | GYBN | 19 | A1 | BN |
| 10 | B2 | BNGN | | | |

- VERIFIED: the table, from the manual 1006672-EN-06 (pages 24-25, URL
  above). Pins 8 and 17 have no terminal. The colours are those of the Pilz
  accessory cable only.
- VERIFIED: entry side of 6M000007 (left) and 6M000009 (front) from the Pilz
  and distributor product pages. 6M000008 = right is deduced from the
  variant list.
- ASSUMED: the three variants share this wiring (the manual gives one table
  for the series). The LDFXX variant has another wiring and is not used.

### 570480 PSEN ml 1.1 actuator

VERIFIED: passive coded actuator, no electrical terminals (Pilz e-shop,
https://www.pilz.com/en-INT/eshop/product/570480). The single terminal
`MECH` is ASSUMED: a marker that lets the actuator sit on a schematic and
pass `--check-elements`, which warns about elements without terminals.

## Not verified

- Pin-to-colour order of 630311.
- Colours and far end of C1000073 / C1000074.
- Same wiring for the three 6M00000x variants.
- Marker terminal of 570480.
- The face of the 19-pin M23 connector is drawn as a numbered box, not with
  the real pin layout, which was not found.
- pilz.com answered 403 to scripted access: its data was read from search
  results of its pages and from mirrored data sheets and manuals. The
  manual pages on manualslib were rebuilt from page text.

`qelectrotech --check-elements extra-elements/19_pilz --grid`.
